# Nigerian agribusiness assessment research

Reviewed 7 November 2026. The engine is an enterprise screening and planning tool. It does not infer market prices, revenue, yields or returns from a user's available capital.

| Reference | How it informs the assessment |
| --- | --- |
| [TMAN Grower Types](https://quiz.modernagriculturenetwork.com/) | Makes its route categories and deliverables clear before payment. Only the public offer was reviewed. |
| [University of Maine farm self-assessment](https://extension.umaine.edu/new-farmers/self-assessment/) | Ask about practical readiness and constraints. Avoid presenting a score as proof of fit. |
| [USDA Farm Loan Assistance Tool](https://www.farmers.gov/loans) | Use eligibility checks to route people and give a clear next step. US loan criteria do not apply in Nigeria. |
| [Teagasc farm business planning](https://teagasc.ie/rural-economy/farm-management/financial-analysis/farm-business-planning/) | Separate the operating plan from its financial viability. Ask about current resources, extra cost, time and risks. |
| [FAO RuralInvest](https://elearning.fao.org/course/view.php?id=757) and [Penn State enterprise budgeting](https://extension.psu.edu/budgeting-for-agricultural-decision-making) | Check setup and working capital separately and show how changes in assumptions affect the decision. |
| [NEPC readiness and registration](https://nepc.gov.ng/ereg/exporter) | Use buyer, destination, pricing, documentation and funding as distinct export checks. |
| [IITA enterprise incubation](https://youthagripreneurs.org/enable-taat/project-value-chains/) and [IITA processing support](https://iita.org/crop-processing/) | Include crops, planting material, fish, inputs, mechanisation and value addition rather than farming alone. |
| [WorldFish value-chain assessment](https://digitalarchive.worldfishcenter.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/829e9258-1639-4deb-922e-33269eeef4bb/content) | Treat grow-out as dependent on reliable inputs, operations and buyers. |
| [FAO palm oil processing](https://www.fao.org/4/y4355e/y4355e00.htm) | Differentiate toll processing, ownership and packaging. Assess throughput, extraction, water and waste. |
| [ICCO cocoa processing](https://www.icco.org/processing-cocoa/) | Distinguish bean-to-bar from buying chocolate for finishing or commissioning a manufacturer. |
| [NAFDAC micro-scale food guidance](https://nafdac.gov.ng/food/guideline-for-micro-scale-enterprises/) | Give food ventures a registration, hygiene and labelling verification step before sales. Confirm rules for the specific product and facility. |

The paid report provides a ranked shortlist, prerequisites, capital comparison where local quotes were supplied, and a 30/60/90-day plan. Naira and dollar amounts are compared only within the buyer's selected currency. The 10% stress case is illustrative. Before marketing performance claims, review reports with Nigerian operators and prospective buyers across several regions and business stages. Compare the usefulness of the reports through task-based feedback.

## Capital evidence for palm oil and chocolate

A budget sum is not an installed or operating cost estimate. The questionnaire asks whether the user is costing a single pilot or recurring production, whether they own the processing operation or contract it out, and whether itemised written quotes support the input. These requirements are checked separately from arithmetic.

- [Selmi professional tempering machines](https://www.selmi-group.com/professional-chocolate-tempering-machines.html) lists a 12 kg temperer at €8,500. That is one equipment example, before import, installation or other equipment. It cannot support a universal startup floor.
- [Guance palm oil machinery](https://www.guanceoilrefiningplant.com/palm-oil-processing-machine-price-in-nigeria-2026/) publishes a US$95,800–US$300,800 range for complete 1–5 tonne/hour lines. The supplier's scope differs from artisanal presses, toll milling and installed Nigerian facilities. Obtain local written quotes for the intended throughput.
- [NAFDAC facility inspection guidance](https://nafdac.gov.ng/our-services/gmp-inspections/) informs the separate facility and compliance cost checks.

No manufacturer quote was obtained for the fictional ₦2 million chocolate example. It is now a preparation case, not evidence of an affordable startup. Future Nigeria-specific price bands should record dated quotes from multiple suppliers by location, throughput and scope, and distinguish equipment-only quotes from installed costs and working capital.
